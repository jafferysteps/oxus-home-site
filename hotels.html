The Challenge

The quiet problem with hotel linen

Every hotelier knows it: linen that feels right at check-in and looks tired by month three. Commercial laundering is brutal — high temperatures, industrial chemistry, relentless cycles. Most contract linen is bought on price and replaced on schedule. The guest notices before the P&L does.

Photography: housekeeping trolley / laundry detail
Our Answer

Engineered for the wash, specified for the guest

We build for the industrial laundry first: no poppers, no buttons, no fiddly fastenings to fail — construction and fibre choices tested against real commercial laundering conditions.

Then we specify for the guest: the weave, the hand-feel, the weight that makes a room feel considered. You choose the standard; we hold it, wash after wash.
