Our Story

The quiet river under a good night’s sleep.

Oxus Home is a UK textile manufacturer founded on a simple frustration: contract bedding is bought like a commodity and felt like one too.

Why We Exist

The company we couldn’t find

Hospitality and care buyers were being asked to choose between two bad options: premium linen priced for flagship hotels, or commodity stock that looked fine on a spreadsheet and tired on a bed.

Nobody was making properly — to order, to spec, in the UK — at a standard both a guest and a procurement manager could respect. So we built the company we couldn’t find.

How We Work

Made to order, on principle

Making to order isn’t a constraint we tolerate — it’s the point. It means no warehouse of compromises, no “close enough” sizing, and no minimum order invented to suit a container.

It means the linen on your beds was made because you asked for it, to the spec you asked for.

You’ll notice a single unbroken line running through our identity. It’s how we think about what we make: one continuous standard, from the first fibre to the made bed.

Quiet, constant, and always moving toward the same place — a good night’s sleep.
