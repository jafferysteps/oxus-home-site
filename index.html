Most contract linen changes hands three times before it reaches a bed. Ours changes hands once. Cut, stitched and quality-checked in our own UK facility — which is why we can make to order, size to spec, and answer for every seam.

Specified for the settings you run

Hospitality and care textiles carry real obligations — fire-safety specification, skin-safe materials, laundering that holds at thermal-disinfection temperatures. We build to the specification your risk assessment demands, and we put it in writing.
