About

A United Kingdom buying office for hospitality textiles.

Oxus Home is a United Kingdom-based service provider and buying office — not a manufacturer. We manage sourcing, vendor selection, quality assurance and delivery for home textiles, hospitality linens and towels, working through a network of manufacturing partners across Asia and Europe. We hold no stock, and we run no factories of our own.

Why We Exist

The gap in the middle

Buyers come to us because the middle ground is where a programme goes wrong. A specification that reads well can still fail after forty commercial washes. A price that looks competitive can hide a vendor that cannot hold a size tolerance across a second production run. Our work is to close that gap before it reaches your housekeeping team.

Every enquiry is quoted individually against the specification, quantity and delivery window you give us. We publish no prices and no minimum order quantities, because both depend on what is being made and where.

How We Work

From scope to delivery note, in five stages

  • 01 · Scope You give us the sector, the product line and the specification or effect you are after. We match resources to that brief rather than pulling off a shelf.
  • 02 · Vendor selection We select by capability, not convenience. If a specification pushes against what is technically producible, we work through that with the vendor before it reaches your production run.
  • 03 · Production & quality Senior, experienced technicians work on the ground through every stage of production, checking against the agreed specification.
  • 04 · Delivery Order execution is coordinated end to end, so goods arrive against the date the programme was built around, on time.
  • 05 · Ongoing relationship A sourcing issue is a call, not a new procurement process. We stay on a programme after delivery and treat every reorder as a continuation of the same relationship.
You’ll notice a single unbroken line running through our identity. It’s how we think about a programme: one continuous standard, from the first specification to the final delivery note.

Quiet, constant, and always moving toward the same place — a good night’s sleep.
