Compliance

Built to the specification your setting requires

  • Fire safety Bedding available specified to BS 7175 (Crib 5) — the standard practical means of satisfying a care-home fire-risk assessment under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. Higher ignition-source specifications available where your assessment requires them.
  • Inherent, not just treated Where FR performance matters, we advise on inherently flame-retardant constructions that hold their rating through repeated thermal-disinfection laundering, rather than topical treatments that can wash out.
  • Waterproof & wipe-clean Discreet waterproof membranes and wipe-clean finishes that protect mattresses without the crackle and heat of old-style institutional covers.
  • Infection-control-friendly Constructions specified to launder at thermal-disinfection temperatures without losing shape or softness.
  • Dementia-considered design Calm, tonal palettes with attention to contrast where it aids orientation.
Content pending review by a qualified fire-safety / compliance advisor. Certification documents are supplied with quotes where held.
Made-to-Order Sizing

Sized for care beds, not just standard ones

Profiling beds, bariatric beds, non-standard mattress depths — care settings are full of beds that high-street sizes don’t fit. Because we make to order, we cut to your actual beds. Send us the dimensions; we’ll do the rest.

Skin Safety

Residents live in this bedding. We specify materials for direct, prolonged skin contact and choose fibres and finishes accordingly.
